# LibreShot 1.3.25

- 框选时在指针左下方显示放大预览，蓝色十字标出落点。
- 同时显示坐标和颜色。`Shift` 切换 RGB/HEX，`⌘C` 复制色值。完成截图后 `⌘C` 仍复制截图。
- 全屏虚线仍可在设置里开关。

## English

- While selecting a region, a loupe sits below and left of the pointer and marks the point with a blue cross.
- It also shows the coordinate and color. Shift switches RGB/HEX, and Command-C copies the value. After the shot is committed, Command-C still copies the screenshot.
- The full-screen dashed guides remain optional.

The accepted package reports 1.3.24 (build 63). It was not rebuilt to change that version string. Remote-desktop guide checks from build 56 were not repeated. Ad-hoc signing; upgrades may require renewed permissions.
