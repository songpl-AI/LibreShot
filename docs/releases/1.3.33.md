# LibreShot 1.3.33

- 框选后可从工具栏切换当前截图的圆角／直角。保存、复制和贴图使用同一角样式。
- 区域截图的放大镜取色后，按 `⌘C` 复制色值并退出。
- 区域截图支持右键取消，框选前后均可用。
- 修复 macOS 27 上普通贴图无法拖动的问题。

## English

- Switch the current capture between rounded and square corners from the toolbar. Saving, copying and pinning all use the selected style.
- Press `⌘C` to copy the sampled color and exit area capture.
- Right-click to cancel area capture before or after selecting a region.
- Fixed pinned images that could not be dragged on macOS 27.
