# LibreShot 1.3.19

- 贴图显示在截图原位置，截图编辑期间默认 `⌘P` 直接贴图；普通滚轮／⌘＋滚轮缩放，Ctrl＋滚轮调整透明度。
- 右键原地编辑贴图，保留撤销；支持编辑时空白移动、抓手移动、空白双击保存，以及箭头／Esc 收起工具栏。
- 工具栏支持左／居中／右对齐；属性滑块支持悬停滚轮调整与提示。
- 修复调整属性后首次绘制需额外点击、贴图编辑工具栏对齐及小数选区马赛克底图偏移。

最近截图召回与历史翻页暂缓。包含 1.3.17／1.3.18 试用改动。

## English

- Pin at the capture position with the default `⌘P` editing shortcut. Zoom with the wheel or Cmd+wheel; adjust opacity with Ctrl+wheel.
- Edit pins in place through the context menu, retain undo, move through blank space or the toolbar handle, double-click blank space to save, and collapse the toolbar with its arrow or Esc.
- Add left/center/right toolbar alignment and wheel adjustment with hover hints for property sliders.
- Fix the extra click before drawing after property changes, pin toolbar alignment and mosaic image offsets in fractional selections.

Recent-capture recall and history browsing are deferred. Includes the 1.3.17/1.3.18 trial changes.
